# Astronautica 1.0.1

Triangulum (M33) was missing from the Local Group, and the Sun beacon did not
match the Orion Spur in the Milky Way artwork. This patch corrects both and
makes clicking our Sun return to the solar-system overview.

- Local Group now shows Milky Way, Andromeda and Triangulum, with labels and
  focus controls. M33 uses an ESO/Digitized Sky Survey 2 telescope image.
- Replaced the inaccurate Milky Way artwork with NASA/JPL's annotated artist
  map. The Sun marker matches its Orion Spur location, between Sagittarius and
  Perseus, about 26,000 light-years from the center.
- Click the Sun marker or its label to go home. Dragging continues to orbit;
  open overlays prevent clicks from reaching the map.
- Galaxy maps stay still for readable labels. Labels adapt to smaller windows.
  Updated offline help, source credits and documentation.

The Local Group remains a **schematic of its three major spiral galaxies**.
Dwarf galaxies are not modeled, and galaxy sizes, spacing and orientations are
illustrative. The Milky Way map is an artist's interpretation of our galaxy.

## Downloads

Install **Astronautica-1.0.1-Windows-x64-Setup.exe** over the existing installation,
or extract the complete **Astronautica-1.0.1-Windows-x64-Portable.zip** into a new
folder. Windows 10 21H1+ / Windows 11 x64. The installer is unsigned; package
checksums are in **SHA256SUMS.txt**.

Original code and documentation remain MIT licensed. Telescope images, supplied
art, generated music and the Unity runtime retain their own terms in
[THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](https://github.com/XayerMorgan/galactic_view/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Validation

745 native player checks and 844 installer checks passed with zero failures.
Checks cover the source-image Sun anchor, all three galaxies, M33 focus,
Sun marker/label navigation, drag cancellation, overlay input isolation,
responsive labels, gallery/audio regressions, clean installation, reinstall,
payload hashes and uninstall preserving user-owned files. Rendered views were
reviewed at 1920×1080 and 1280×720.

Manual Windows mouse checks also confirmed that clicking the Sun opens the
solar-system overview, clicking Triangulum's label focuses M33, and dragging
from the Sun rotates the map without changing sectors.
