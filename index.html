Cosmic Zoom Engine Specification

This 3D visualization models four distinct scales of the observable universe with real-time relativistic transit mathematics ($c = 299,792.458\text{ km/s}$):

Stage 1: Solar System (~8.33 Light-Hours)
Sun to Neptune heliocentric model highlighting planetary orbital planes.
Stage 2: Milky Way Galaxy (~100,000 Light-Years)
150,000-particle barred spiral disk showing Orion Arm, Sun's beacon, and Sagittarius A*.
Stage 3: Local Group Cluster (~10 Million Light-Years)
Milky Way, Andromeda (M31), Triangulum (M33), and dwarf satellites in mutual gravitational filament.
Stage 4: Cosmic Web & Particle Horizon (~93 Billion Light-Years)
Filamentary dark matter/galaxy web, cosmic voids, and the Observable Universe CMB Horizon sphere.

Audio features ElevenLabs scientific narration voiced by George with cinematic acoustic SFX. Use Left Click + Drag to Orbit, Right Click to Pan, and Scroll to continuously Zoom.
