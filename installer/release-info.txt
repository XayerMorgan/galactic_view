Welcome to Astronautica

Explore four cosmic scales, orient yourself beneath a local horizon, and browse
all 110 Messier objects plus 12 deep-sky highlights in an offline image gallery.

This installer installs for your Windows account without administrator access.
The complete app, soundtrack, image gallery and field guide are included.
Press F1 in the app or use the Start menu Field Guide shortcut for help.

The MIT license does not relicense those assets. Image credits are also shown

Coordinates and distances are educational approximations. The Local Group
view shows Milky Way, Andromeda and Triangulum. Dwarf galaxies are not modeled.
Click our Sun in the Milky Way map to return to the solar system.
