ASTRONAUTICA - COSMIC EXPLORER

Run CosmicZoomEngine.exe. Keep all files and folders in this package together.
No Unity editor or internet connection is needed to explore.

HELP: press F1 in the app, select Settings > Help & field guide, or open:
CosmicZoomEngine_Data/StreamingAssets/Help/index.html

FIRST FLIGHT
1-4: cosmic sectors. Drag: look/orbit. Mouse wheel: zoom. R: reset view.
S: observatory. G: photograph gallery. T: guided tour. H: interface visibility.
Escape closes overlays, leaves the observatory, or exits the app.
Set your location in Sky > Change observer location for altitude/azimuth.
